Paper Reframe

When the framing itself is the question.

A paper can be technically sound yet organized around a weaker question than its evidence can support. Reframe examines that whole-paper choice without pretending that every paper needs a new story.

01

Test the current framing

Steelman the paper’s existing view, then assess whether it is already sufficient or leaves important evidence and tensions unexplained.

02

Explore genuinely different views

Compare alternative whole-paper viewpoints—not cosmetic title or introduction rewrites—against the manuscript’s actual evidence.

03

Set an evidence budget

Separate what the current manuscript can support from hypotheses that require bounded revision or open-ended new research.

What returns

Two PDFs: a Framing Memo with the strongest working view, and a Research Plan with evidence-bounded next steps.

Start the email subject with [Reframe]. The default is Mode: bounded; you may instead request no-new-experiments or open in the message body.
