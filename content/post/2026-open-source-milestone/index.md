As of May 2026, our combined open-source research ecosystem spans 67 public repositories with 10K+ GitHub stars and 800+ forks.

The collection includes generation and foundation-model systems such as [Lotus](https://github.com/EnVision-Research/Lotus), [LucidDreamer](https://github.com/EnVision-Research/LucidDreamer), [Kiss3DGen](https://github.com/EnVision-Research/Kiss3DGen), [DriveRecon](https://github.com/EnVision-Research/DriveRecon), [ComfyMind](https://github.com/EnVision-Research/ComfyMind), [DVD](https://github.com/EnVision-Research/DVD), and [TiViBench](https://github.com/EnVision-Research/TiViBench), together with datasets, benchmarks, model weights, demos, and evaluation tools.

The ecosystem is used beyond our own repositories through Hugging Face models and Spaces, community ComfyUI integrations, and downstream research projects.
