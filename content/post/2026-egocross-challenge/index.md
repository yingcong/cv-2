The official challenge report, released on August 5, documents DomainWiseInfer's first-place results in both the Source-Limited and Open-Source tracks of the CVPR 2026 EgoCross Challenge.

The challenge focuses on cross-domain egocentric video question answering. I served as a faculty mentor to the team.
