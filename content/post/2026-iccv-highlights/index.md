Two of our papers were selected as Highlight papers at ICCV 2025:

- [PRM: Photometric Stereo based Large Reconstruction Model](/publication/2025-ge-prm/)
- [RhythmGuassian: Repurposing Generalizable Gaussian Model For Remote Physiological Measurement](/publication/2025-lu-rhythmguassian/)

These works reflect two active directions in our group: large reconstruction models for 3D vision and generalizable visual modeling for physiological measurement.
