Our new preprint, [WorldAlign: Decoupled 4D Reward for World-Consistent Video Generation](/publication/2026-he-worldalign/), was released on October 8.

The work studies how separate feedback for static scenes and dynamic subjects can improve geometric and temporal consistency in generated videos.
